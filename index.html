Thesis & Position
THESIS

A name is not a label added after the system is built. It is the first piece of infrastructure.

Before a system can be discovered, an agent routed, an identity verified, or a namespace resolved, something has to name it — precisely enough to distinguish it, consistently enough to resolve it, and generally enough to extend.

Naming is infrastructure because everything downstream depends on it.

NAMING CHAIN
NAME → IDENTITY → RESOLUTION → ROUTING → TRUST
What begins as a name becomes an addressable identity, then a resolvable object, then something systems can route, verify, and trust.
WHY iXName

IX is intentionally open. It carries the compressed technical character of exchange, indexing, identity, and infrastructure without being locked to one expansion.

That openness is part of the value: the name can sit above more than one naming architecture.
